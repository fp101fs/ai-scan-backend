I walked down to the pier early this morning before the fog lifted. It was freezing. The damp cold cut straight through my wool coat, but the water was dead calm, like glass. A lone fisherman was untangling his line, cursing softly under his breath in Portuguese. I stood there for maybe twenty minutes, watching the harbor lights blink on and off across the channel, until my fingers went numb and I had to head back toward the cafe on 4th Street for a warm coffee.
